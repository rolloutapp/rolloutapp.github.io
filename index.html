Rollout

A backgammon trainer for iPhone.

Make your move or your cube decision, then see how it compares with every other option. 1,000 puzzles checked by computer analysis, plus courses in pip counting and shot counting, every opening roll and reply, and short lessons on strategy and the cube.

Support

Questions, a puzzle you think is wrong, or a problem with the app: email rollout.backgammon@gmail.com. Include the puzzle number (for example R-0123) if it's about a puzzle.

Subscription

Rollout is free to try: 50 puzzles, the Openings trainer, the first lesson of each course and one run of each practice drill. Full access is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel.

  • Cancel: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Rollout. You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: in Rollout, open Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Progress is stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it.
