Privacy Policy

Rollout collects no data.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What Rollout stores

Your answers, scores and settings are saved on your iPhone so the app can track your progress. They never leave your device and we can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What Rollout doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data sent to us or to anyone else. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive no payment details or personal information. The app asks the App Store only whether your subscription is active.

Children

Rollout collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
