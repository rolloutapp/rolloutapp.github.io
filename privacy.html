Privacy Policy

Rollout collects no data.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What Rollout stores

Your answers, scores and settings are saved on your phone so the app can track your progress. They never leave your device and we can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Without a subscription, the app also keeps a short list of the free puzzles and practice runs you've used, so the free allowance isn't reset by reinstalling. On iPhone it's kept in your iCloud Keychain; on Android, in Google's Block Store, which may back it up to your Google account. It contains nothing else.

What Rollout doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data sent to us or to anyone else. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. They process your payment under Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy; we receive no payment details or personal information. The app asks the store only whether your subscription is active.

Children

Rollout collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
